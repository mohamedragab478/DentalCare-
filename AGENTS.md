# Custom Agent Directives

## Design & Engineering System: Anti-AI Slop Rules (Strict Enforcement)
Refer to `/DESIGN_RULES.md` for complete design specifications.

1. **Colors**: Single clinical accent color (`#006194` primary brand). Clean neutrals (`slate`/cool greys). No purple-blue gradients or gradient text anywhere.
2. **Shadows & Flat Depth**: No drop shadows on stationary cards, sections, or list items. Shadows strictly reserved for floating overlays (modals/popovers).
3. **No Decorative Icon Enclosures**: Icons sit cleanly directly on backgrounds at text size. No tinted rounded boxes or pastel badges around icons.
4. **No Sparkles/Emojis in UI**: No ✨, ✦, 🪄, or emoji in navigation, buttons, titles, or badges.
5. **Precise Clinical Copy**: No marketing hype words, no em dashes (—), clear and direct terminology.
6. **Interaction & Motion**: Fast 120-160ms color/border transitions on hover. No floating lift, zoom scales, or glowing blooms.
